Decision support · Practical next steps

What could a team do next?

The analysis helps prioritise what to investigate. It does not prove which intervention will cause satisfaction to improve.

1
Investigate pay experienceReview fairness, communication and employee feedback alongside pay levels.
2
Test training prioritiesEvaluate targeted training options against budget and current workforce needs.
3
Validate locallyTest whether these historical signals hold in the organisation’s current data.

Evidence quality

22/22 independent SQL checks passed, with a maximum R to SQL difference of 0.005.

WERS is observational: association does not establish causation. The identified segment is a group-level signal, not an individual diagnosis. Flexitime missingness was 10.9% and job-sharing missingness was 17.5%, so those measures need particular caution.

Data note

WERS is historical 2011 survey data, not a current workforce forecast. Source microdata is not redistributed; the public repository contains code, documentation and aggregated outputs.
